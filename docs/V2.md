# v2: the new screens (vertical slice)

A rebuild of the UI from scratch. It lives beside the old game and does not touch it: its own entry, its own build, its
own save. Since v2.1 it follows the addendum of `docs/JOBSIM_UI_RESEARCH.md` ("screens actually looked at"), not its
section 3: **one drawn screen per workplace** — no Back room, no room tabs — and the game never suggests ordering.

```bash
npm run dev:v2      # Vite dev server on /v2.html
npm run build:v2    # type-check + build → dist-v2/index.html (single file, opens by double-click)
```

Save key: `minato-v2-save` (the old game's `minato-chabou-save` is never read or written). Menu → 最初からやり直す clears it.

## What is in the slice

- **Start**: English only; ゼロから, every 英検 grade (5級 … 1級) and every TOEIC band (〜400 … 〜990), through the existing
  `applyStartLevel` (assumed-known words are seeded but never confirmed; no meaning is shown for them anywhere).
- **The workplace is one picture** (`ui/scene.tsx`): a stage in fixed design units (desktop 1600×940, phone 400×820),
  scaled to fit and held at the top. 始業前 / 業務中 / 業務後 are states of it: the card on the door / hatch
  (準備中 → 営業中 / 受付中 → 準備中; the player flips it to open, and again to close once the last person has gone), the
  light through the window (morning, the day, dusk with the lamp lit), who stands at the counter (nobody before opening).
  Before opening the day's note is a paper lying on the counter / desk; after closing the day's card lies over the room
  (customers, coins, used, came in — no per-answer marks) with 一日を終える.
- **Tea house** (`ui/tea.tsx`): the counter seen from behind it. Back wall: the door with its sign, the framed town map,
  the market basket on its hook by the door, the window, the shelf of jars (desktop: the wall unit; phone: a shelf along
  the back of the counter, rows that fit every jar with its name). The customer stands at the counter with a big speech
  bubble (29 design units ≈ 23 px desktop, 19 ≈ 18.5 px phone; words tappable). On the counter: the stage's tools, the cup
  and its slip, the serve plate (出す / 次のお客さん in the same place), the order pad by the till, the coin jar.
- **Stock**: every jar / supply carries its count as a badge in the scene, always. 0 shows "0" (red) and the container
  greyed; tapping it says 在庫 0 for a moment and nothing else. The morning note states a 0 as a fact ("We have no
  ginger.") and never says to buy.
- **Ordering**: the order pad by the till (clinic: on the desk), always in the same place, opened only by the player; it
  opens with nothing ticked (rows: drawing, English unit name, unit price, on the shelf now, stepper, line total;
  tiered delivery fee, total, 注文する). During the day the crate arrives about 8 seconds later (a receipt on the spike
  meanwhile), lands by the door with "+N" and the counts go up; ordered after closing it comes with the next morning
  (the crate by the door, "+N" on the jars).
- **Market in person** (tea house, before opening / after closing only, from the basket by the door): cheaper, no fee, an English exchange (pick a
  good, say how many, hear the total in words, pay coins on the tray, get change).
- **Clinic reception** (`ui/clinic.tsx`): the desk seen from the receptionist's chair. The patient stands at the hatch
  (the waiting room behind the glass, the card hanging in it) and talks in the bubble; the supplies (folders, ink,
  message paper, cards) stand in the wall cabinet with their counts; the task's papers lie on the leather blotter; the
  order pad, the receipts and the stamps / hand-in plate are on the right. Every archetype the clinic uses at ranks 1–3
  (sort into file folders, check incl. choosing the right value, respond: reply slips, message pad, appointment book;
  audio-only calls). Each task uses one supply; at 0 the work cannot be handed in, and the desk says only
  「紙ばさみ 在庫 0」.
- **Town** (from the map on the wall): the nine workplaces as signboards (desktop: on a small drawn harbour; phone: a list first, the map behind a
  tab). Tea house and clinic can be entered ("今日はここで働く" before opening, "明日はここで働く" after closing); the
  other seven say 移行中.
- **Top edge**: the day, the part of the day, (desktop) where, coins, the one notebook button, the menu.
- **Notebook**: one book, four tabs (言葉 / 単語帳 / 検証 / 記録). Desktop: a column beside the stage (the stage
  rescales, nothing is covered); phone: a bottom sheet under the bubble. Tapping any word in the world opens its
  page there (own meaning, memo, the sentences it was met in, star → 単語帳). The player's own meaning is written small
  under the word everywhere it appears.
- **No verdicts**: only the person's line (built by the existing reaction / explain code) and their face; the summary
  lists customers, coins, used and received goods — never how each answer went.

## Architecture

```
v2.html, vite.v2.config.ts          entry + build (single file, renamed to dist-v2/index.html)
src/v2/main.tsx                     loads the compiled English lists, renders the app
src/v2/data.ts                      the English GameData (content + indexes), built once
src/v2/logic/                       game logic, no React
  catalog.ts                        what each workplace stocks: unit phrase, portions per unit, price; fee tiers;
                                    start stock / money; what a cup pays
  stock.ts                          pure stock arithmetic: quote, place an order (pay now, arrive later), receive due
                                    deliveries, buy in person, reserve / use portions, shortfall for the morning note
  state.ts                          V2State (the save), load / save / parse
  day.ts                            the day: new game, morning, open, serve a cup, next customer, close, end the day,
                                    town choice, market, the clinic shift; deliveries (tick)
  stock.test.ts, day.test.ts        stock arithmetic (buy, deliver, use, carry over, after-closing orders, refusals),
                                    three full days, reload, a 英検2級 start, clinic shifts at ranks 1–3
src/v2/ui/                          React; every screen is new
  store.tsx                         one mutable state + act() (save after each action), layout (desk ≤ 820 px → phone),
                                    reduced motion, delivery timer
  frame.tsx                         top edge (day, part, coins, notebook, menu); dock for town / market
  scene.tsx                         the scaled stage, the light, and the objects both workplaces share: door sign,
                                    count badge, order pad + receipts, crate, town map, market basket, day's note,
                                    the day's card, speech bubble
  bits.tsx                          tappable words (+ own meanings), reading card, drawn icons, listen button
  tea.tsx, clinic.tsx               the two workplaces (one screen each, desktop and phone geometry)
  order.tsx, market.tsx, town.tsx, notebook.tsx, start.tsx
  art.tsx                           new drawings: desk supplies, crate, door, stall awning, harbour, town map
src/v2/v2.css                       tokens, both layouts, reduced motion
```

The whole save is one object: the existing `LangProgress` (words, SRS, own meanings, 単語帳, 検証, difficulty, jobs'
ranks, the shop's stage / reputation / returning customers) plus v2's own day (`day.part`, `day.room` = 'shop' | 'town' |
'market', `day.workplace`; save version 2 — a version-1 save's 'back' / 'front' load as 'shop'), stock per workplace, pending deliveries, recent arrivals, the cup in progress and the day's tally.

### Reused unchanged

- Content: `src/content/**` (orders, templates, reactions, customers, market phrases and number words, the clinic).
- Shop engine: `startDay` / `serve` / `nextCustomer` / `endDay` (planning, rules, reactions, reputation, stage, SRS,
  returning customers) — v2 only adds stock around it.
- Jobs engine: `planShift`, `beginShift`, `submitTask`, `nextTask`, `checkRankUp`, reactions that say what was off
  (`explain.ts`), near-miss replies, `solveTask` (tests).
- Core: tokenizer, SRS, verification pages, word book / own meanings, difficulty (start levels, assumed-known words,
  metrics hooks), levels report, curriculum lists.
- Illustrations: `ContainerArt`, `CustomerArt` (+ `lookFor`, `expressionFor`), `VesselArt`, the tool drawings,
  the shop's `WindowGroup` / `DoorGroup` / `DoorBellGroup` / `ShelfUnitGroup` / `LampGroup`, `skyAt`, `DecorArt`,
  `TeaSetArt`, `CoinJarArt`, `BasketArt` and `ArtDefs` from `src/ui/art`; `brewing.ts` (cup colour / pieces) and `speech.ts` (TTS) as helpers. No old component,
  layout or stylesheet is imported.

### New logic (where the old logic fought the design)

- Stock is counted in portions on every shelf item (old: only market goods, shown as 残りN / 品切れ). A purchase adds
  exactly `units × per`; the stock never resets at a day boundary; portions in the cup are reserved until served.
- Ordering is a form with a fee, not the market scene; arrival is a timed crate (or the next morning).
- The day's flow (parts, the town choice) is v2's own; the old `activities/day.ts` segment planner is not used.
- The clinic's desk supplies.

## Known gaps

- With the notebook open on desktop the stage rescales to the narrower width (text gets smaller, more counter below).
- The scene is drawn for these two workplaces only; the counter / desk of the other jobs needs its own picture.
- Story (regulars' beats, letters, chapters, the night ledger), renovation catalogue, the house delivery, difficulty
  settings after the start, sound effects, settings (voice, guess display) and Chinese are not in v2.
- The tea house's rank shown in the strip is derived from its stage (the old game derives it from cups and words).
- Old saves are not imported.
- A clinic day skips the tea house; its stage clock still moves (the old game holds it back).

## Porting the other seven jobs

The framework does the heavy lifting: every job is data over the archetypes, and v2's desk already renders sort,
check and respond (choose / form / slot) for any job's tasks. Per job:

1. **Paper desks** (town hall, newspaper, academy library, trading office): add the workplace id to `WorkplaceId`,
   a supplies catalogue (3–4 items and what each archetype uses), its name in `PLACE`, start stock; generalise
   `Clinic` (and its geometry) to take the job id (they only read `jobById(data, 'clinic')` and the supply table).
   Roughly half a day each, mostly tuning (bin labels length, long documents at rank 4–5, the slot layout `rooms`).
2. **Inn and post office** add the **trade** archetype (price board, goods drawers, coins, till, the letter scale and
   rate card) — a new surface, about 1–2 days, then each job like a paper desk.
3. **Harbour kitchen** needs the **make** archetype (pantry with counts — it maps naturally onto v2's stock —, stations,
   vessels, recipe cards with order and "if there is no …"), about 2–3 days.
4. Evening second shifts (`hours` with 2) need a small extension of the day (`part` 2 → work elsewhere → home).

Systems: story and the night ledger 3–5 days (the day's card after closing is the natural ledger; story cards should
be papers lying on the counter / desk, never overlays); renovation 2–3 days (catalogue as another form on the desk;
effects mostly exist in `src/renovation`); difficulty settings and 学習の記録 1–2 days (logic exists); Chinese 1–2 days
for the tea house (content exists; the catalogue's unit phrases and the market's number words come from zh content,
CJK typography); the house delivery 2 days.
